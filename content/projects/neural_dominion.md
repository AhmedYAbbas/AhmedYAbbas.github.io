# Neural Dominion

Neural Dominion is an upcoming cyberpunk 4X turn-based strategy game from GamesBond Studios (published by Camlann Games) — players lead one of five rival gangs fighting for control of Technograd, blending city-building and economic strategy with turn-based tactical combat.

I worked on this as part of a 4-person team, building the event system, UI implementation, the stats system, and the building system.
