April 5, 2024 · Me

City Building (GitHub)#

A work-in-progress city building game prototype in UE5.3. It’s a recreation for The Game Dev Channel’s City Building Playlist but using C++ instead of Blueprints.

Features#

  • Camera controls (move, orbit, pitch, zoom, free-roam).
  • Mouse cursor, click objects in-world, and highlight material.
  • Grid and buildings and roads placement system.
  • HUD for selecting buildings and roads.
  • Day and night cycle.
  • Currency system.
  • Random citizens generation.
  • Unreal Engine
  • C++
